# 👕 In-Game Skin Changer & Animated Capes

BoostClient includes a built-in 3D wardrobe system allowing players to upload custom skins and animated GIF capes with multiplayer synchronization.

---

## 🎨 3-Slot Skin Wardrobe

Every player has 3 dedicated skin slots:
- Upload any standard 64x64 or 128x128 PNG skin.
- Switch active slots at any time with a single click.
- Change skins directly in-game by clicking the **Skins & Capes** button on the PLAY stage or in the Wardrobe modal.

---

## 🎽 Animated GIF Cape Support

Unlike traditional launchers that restrict users to static PNG capes, BoostClient supports animated GIF capes:
- Upload any `.gif` cape file or select from default animated designs (Fire, Rainbow, Creeper, Angel, Dragon, Galaxy).
- Capes render in real-time on your character back with fluid physics.

---

## 🌐 Multiplayer Visibility with BoostX

- When you play on any server, other players using **BoostClient** with the **BoostX mod** will automatically see your custom skin and animated cape.
- Servers equipped with the **BoostX Server Engine** (`boostserver-paper-1.0.0.jar`) synchronize these cosmetics to all clients over the network.

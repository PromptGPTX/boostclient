# 🚀 Getting Started with BoostClient

This guide explains how to install and launch BoostClient on your system.

---

## 📥 Download

Grab the official installer from GitHub Releases:
- **Installer**: [`boostclient-setup.exe`](https://github.com/PromptGPTX/boostclient/releases/latest/download/boostclient-setup.exe)
- **Direct Version 1.2.0**: [`boostclient-setup-1.2.0.exe`](https://github.com/PromptGPTX/boostclient/releases/download/v1.2.0/boostclient-setup-1.2.0.exe)

---

## 🛠️ Step 1: Account Setup

When you first launch BoostClient, you will be guided through the **Account** tab:

1. Click **+ ADD ACCOUNT** or click **Add Account** in the header.
2. Select your login type:
   - **Offline / Cracked Mode**: Type any username and click **Add Offline Account**.
   - **Microsoft OAuth**: Click **Sign In with Microsoft** to authorize your official Mojang account.
3. Your account card will appear in the grid and become selected.

---

## ⛏️ Step 2: Create a Minecraft Profile

After creating your account:

1. Navigate to the **PROFILES** tab.
2. Click **+ CREATE PROFILE**.
3. Choose your desired Minecraft version (e.g., `1.20.4`, `1.21.1`, or `26.3`) and mod loader (`Fabric`, `Forge`, `NeoForge`, or `Vanilla`).
4. Select your RAM allocation (Default: 4 GB).
5. Click **Save Profile**.

---

## 🎮 Step 3: Launch Minecraft

1. Head to the **PLAY** tab.
2. Verify your active profile in the selector pill.
3. Click the bold orange **LAUNCH GAME** button.
4. BoostClient will automatically resolve assets, libraries, and native binaries with a live progress card.
5. Minecraft launches automatically!
